Ember Duel is an original card duel that plays offline in a desktop browser: you and a rival AI trade cards from four houses — Flame, Tide, Leaf and Spark — spending energy to strike, guard and heal, with a house-bond bonus for playing two cards of the same house in one turn. First to bring the other to 0 life wins the duel.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 and press "Deal the cards". Click a card or press 1–7 to play it, Space ends your turn, R starts a new duel after a win or a loss.
