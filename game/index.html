Ember Duel

A card duel of the four houses. Outplay the rival, guard your ember, and take all 20 of their life.

  1. Each round you gain energy and draw a card. Cards cost energy to play.
  2. Strike cards deal damage, Guard cards raise your shield, Mend cards heal you.
  3. Play two cards of the same house in one turn: the second gains +2 power. That is the house bond.
  4. First to bring the other to 0 life wins the duel.
Mouse and keyboard: 1–7 play a card · Space ends turn · R restarts
